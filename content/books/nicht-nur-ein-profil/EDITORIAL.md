# Nicht nur ein Profil

An original 200-page A2-oriented German reader. Ten chapters of twenty short pages, with four paragraphs on every page. English translations accompany the exact German paragraphs. The plot follows Ben, 29, in the fictional city Lindenstadt, from dating-app disappointment to a lasting relationship with Lea, 28. Lea has her own work, friends, preferences and decisions; she is not a reward for Ben's improvement.

Language follows the Council of Europe's A2 reading-for-leisure descriptor: short, simple stories in familiar, concrete situations and high-frequency everyday language.
