English definitions and grammatical data: English Wiktionary contributors,
via Kaikki / Wiktextract by Tatu Ylonen. Original snapshot: English Wiktionary
2026-09-02 dump, extracted 2026-10-03. Original entry and author history for each
row: `https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/<URL-encoded fifth row field>#German`.
The fifth field retains the original source title, including when an article
or reflexive pronoun is added to the display headword.

Adaptations: selected German lemmas and senses; article/reflexive formatting;
usage/register/spelling/inflection filters; terminal punctuation removal;
targeted English corrections; topic assignment; cross-level deduplication and
editorial ordering. See docs/C1_VOCABULARY.md and the provenance manifest.

data are CC BY-SA 4.0; its software is Apache 2.0. See the upstream source and
acknowledgments: https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq/blob/9e033608b2538615fa4424569c18009a5c303376/NOTICE.md .
A copy of the upstream notice accompanies this snapshot.
