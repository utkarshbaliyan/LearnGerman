# Video course – exercise & retention plan (keep consistent across all 24 episodes)

Updated 25 Nov 2026 after pedagogical & retention review (combining Nicos Weg episodic context, Babbel active recall, Seedlang gender/audio drills, and calm spaced-review habit loops — with zero AI and zero manipulative gamification).

## 1. Core pedagogical rules
Exercises teach German. They never quiz plot trivia.
- Every item makes the learner **understand or produce German**.
- Zero plot/character trivia (no "who is from where?", "who ordered coffee?").
- **Strict grammar ceiling:** never require grammar the course hasn't taught yet (e.g., no accusative in E01–E06, no dative rules before E13). Fixed chunks taught in the video (`zum Bahnhof`, `mit der U-Bahn`) are fine as chunks.
- **Active recall > passive clicking:** limit pure `choice` sets to ≤ 40 % of an episode. Pair recognition (`choice`, `match`, `sort`) with active production (`gap`, `type`, `fix`, `order`, `chat`, `form`, `write`, `speak`).
- **Video as rescue, not gate:** every core set has `at` (`m:ss`) pointing to where Frau Weber teaches it; wrong answers offer `Rewatch m:ss`.
- **Lean, crisp volume:** aim for **80–115 high-value items per episode** (plus optional `bonus: true` extra sets) so learners feel momentum instead of worksheet fatigue.

## 2. Episode structure: 4 steps + focused micro-sessions
The top-level roadmap stays predictable across all 24 episodes so beginners never re-learn navigation:
1. **Words** — Flashcards + collapsed word list on top, followed by 4–5 focused sets (audio recognition, tactile `match` or `sort`, topic drill, English → German typing). Extra/website words live in an optional `bonus: true` section.
2. **Grammar** — Grammar board on top, followed by 4–5 focused sets (tactile `sort` or `gap` forms, `chat` or picture `talk`, `fix` the sentence, `order` word-order tiles).
3. **Exam skills** — Styled real-life German artifacts (`Lesen` realia), `Hören`, `Diktat`, interactive `chat` roleplay or `Schreiben 1` form, `Schreiben 2` free text (live checklist + mistake warnings), and `Sprechen` (record + model).
4. **Test** — 15 mixed, production-heavy items. Pass mark **80 %**. Mistakes automatically land in the episode's Review queue until fixed.

### Micro-session UX (anti-fatigue)
- Inside each step, **show one exercise set at a time** (`Set 2 of 5` + numbered stepper pills + `Next set →`), with a `Show all` toggle for learners who prefer a full-page view.
- `bonus: true` sections sit in a separate **Extra practice** area so core completion stays fast and clear.

## 3. Exercise toolkit (11 mechanics)
Mix these across episodes so **no two consecutive episodes feel like the same worksheet**:

| Type | What it does | Best used for |
|---|---|---|
| `match` | Tap-to-pair cards (German ↔ English, opposites, Q&A) | Vocabulary pairs, opposites (`weit ↔ nah`), phrase ↔ reply |
| `sort` | Sort 6–10 cards into 2–3 color-coded buckets | `der / die / das`, `ei vs. ie`, `du vs. Sie`, `nicht vs. kein`, `haben vs. sein`, food categories |
| `chat` | 3–5 turn realistic conversation with character avatar + Qwen voice | Real-life roleplays (station directions, checkout, ordering food, making plans, phone calls) |
| `choice` | 3-option multiple choice (with optional audio) | *Hören*, *Lesen* (Richtig/Falsch), quick discrimination |
| `gap` | Inline fill-in-the-blank (optional hint) | Verb endings, articles, case endings, prepositions |
| `type` | Full-word or sentence typing (with ä ö ü ß bar) | *Diktat*, English → German recall, picture *Ask them* (`du`/`Sie`) |
| `fix` | Strike-through mistake → type corrected German (case-sensitive) | Classic learner traps (`nicht ein` → `kein`, Verb-2, capitals) |
| `order` | Tap word tiles into sentence order | German word order (Verb 2, W-questions, separable verbs, *Perfekt* bracket) |
| `form` | Mini real-life form fields | *Schreiben Teil 1* (Anmeldung, hotel card, travel card, appointment slip) |
| `write` | Free German writing with live regex checklist + mistake warnings + model | *Schreiben Teil 2* (short messages, emails, descriptions) |
| `speak` | Browser mic recording + playback + native Qwen model | *Sprechen* (self-intro, asking/giving info, making requests) |

## 4. Realia styles for *Lesen* & *Schreiben 1*
`ReadingText` accepts `style` (+ optional `meta`) so reading tasks feel like authentic German everyday documents:
- `sign` — station notices, doctor/shop opening hours, city signs (E05, E06, E14)
- `receipt` — supermarket bill, bakery/shop prices (E07, E12)
- `menu` — café / restaurant menu (E01, E08)
- `chat` — WhatsApp / SMS group chat (E03, E11, E19)
- `email` — short formal/informal email or invitation (E10, E16, E22)
- `ad` — flat listing, job poster, event flyer (E04, E15, E21)
- `card` — ID/registration/travel/hotel card (E02, E20, E24)
- `notice` — default warm paper note

## 6. Calm retention loop (why learners come back)
Shown at the top of `/video-course` via `CourseDashboard` — built strictly around competence and clarity (no XP, gems, leaderboards, or punitive streak resets):
- **1-click Resume:** remembers the learner's last episode & step (`Continue Episode 6 · Grammar →`).
- **Clear A1 Roadmap Meter:** episodes passed out of 24 + total saved mistakes across episodes with direct links to clear them.
- **Spaced Review Nudges:** when a passed episode is ≥ 2 days old, surfaces a calm *"Ready for a 2-min refresh"* card.
- **14-day Consistency Strip:** quiet dot calendar showing active study days (misses never wipe out past progress).

## 7. UI & build rules
- Colours strictly from `platform-colours.css` theme tones (Words sky, Grammar lilac, Exam skills butter, Test sage), supporting light + dark modes.
- Flashcards: "Add to review" + Show / Next only; word list collapsed inside Words step and synced with `/vocabulary`.
- One downloadable PDF per episode (all 11 exercise types + answer key + word list): `.local-piper/render-venv/bin/python scripts/build-video-course-pdf.py`.
- All audio = local Qwen3-TTS voices (browser speech only as fallback).

→ `node scripts/build-video-course-audio.mjs` → render missing voices (`qwen_voices.py needed_web.json`)
