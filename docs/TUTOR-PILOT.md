# A2 tutor pilot: draft evaluation pack

Status: unreviewed task drafts. A German teacher must calibrate task difficulty and scoring before recruitment. The app includes the speaking missions; writing equivalents below are an evaluation protocol, not additional app routes.

Use the same communicative goal in each pair. Counterbalance which mode learners do first; use different factual details to reduce rehearsal. Collect a first unaided response, then one repair with the tutor. At least seven days later use the unfamiliar transfer task without hints or model answers. Do not show transfer prompts in advance.

| Pair | Speaking goal | Writing task | Unfamiliar delayed task |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Reschedule an appointment | Email a language school: explain your absence, suggest two times and request confirmation. | Rearrange a volunteer shift with a different reason and schedule. |
| 2 | Find a suitable flat | Message a landlord about your household, routine and viewing availability. | Explain your group's workspace requirements to an office manager. |
| 3 | Report a lost bag | Describe a lost bag, contents, location and collection arrangements. | Report a missing parcel with distinguishing details and delivery instructions. |
| 4 | Explain a travel problem | Tell railway support what happened, what you tried and what help you need. | Explain an unsuccessful online booking to customer support. |
| 5 | Meet a visiting friend | Send directions and a backup meeting point from the station. | Guide a courier to a different entrance and arrange an alternative drop-off. |
| 6 | Correct a restaurant order | Write a polite message about an unsuitable meal and request a replacement. | Explain why a proposed activity is unsuitable and suggest an alternative. |
| 7 | Choose a present together | Message a friend: name the recipient, compare gifts and assign the purchase. | Arrange shared supplies for a club event with named recipients. |
| 8 | Join a volunteer group | Introduce two volunteers, explain their abilities and ask about the schedule. | Introduce a project team and allocate responsibilities. |
| 9 | Ask a neighbor for help | Describe a household problem, attempted fixes and requested help. | Explain a problem with equipment to a course organizer. |
| 10 | Plan a weekend outing | Describe last weekend and propose a new plan with transport and timing. | Compare a previous excursion with a plan for visiting relatives. |

Recruit 20–30 A2 learners with recording consent and clear deletion rights. Collect only necessary study data. Use pseudonymous participant IDs and restrict raw samples to authorized reviewers. Set a study retention deadline before collection; app task histories remain until user deletion.

Teacher scoring: communicative task completion (0–3), target construction accuracy (correct opportunities / total opportunities), and whether each tutor correction is valid, unnecessary stylistic advice, or incorrect. Review without seeing the tutor's score first. Double-review a subset and resolve disagreements.

Record per task: participant ID, task/mode, unaided or assisted, help level, revision completion, teacher task-success score, valid error count, false corrections, transcript edits, elapsed time, provider latency and actual provider cost. Measure return after seven days separately from language improvement. Report missing follow-up data and denominator sizes.

Primary outcome: unaided success on unfamiliar delayed tasks. Secondary outcomes: recurring errors per opportunity, repair completion and false-correction rate. Engagement, same-script fluency and maximum one-shot scores are not evidence of transferable improvement. No pronunciation-quality claims are supported by transcript grading.
