# Practical reading and listening pilot

## Delivery order
1. Implement six linked samples: A1 chapters 1 and 9; A2 chapters 4 and 18; B1 chapters 1 and 4. Each has a practical document, an independent listening scenario, evidence-based feedback and account-synced practice results.
2. Include a different reading and listening scenario per sample, available seven days after both initial activities have been checked. Record first exposure, first result, latest result, support and repeated attempts separately. These are practice observations, not proficiency certification.
3. German teacher review of all texts, audio, level placement, translations, distractors and evidence. Pilot with 20–30 learners, including comprehension without support, false or ambiguous answers, delayed performance and audio usability. Obtain participant consent before external data collection; do not contact anyone automatically.
4. Revise these samples using the findings before expanding to the remaining 66 chapters. Add more genres and reviewed human speech progressively; no claim that six samples establish B1 competence.

## Content and audio
A1: course registration card / shopping notice, introductions / market dialogue.
A2: appointment email / travel timetable, voicemail / station announcement.
B1: accommodation comparison / service email, planning dialogue / repair negotiation.
Each delayed task changes people, facts and the scenario rather than recycling a script.

Pilot audio is rendered from installed German synthetic voices into portable audio files. It is labelled synthetic, not teacher-reviewed or a human recording. Exact segment times allow feedback replay. This removes device-voice variability but does not replace review of pronunciation, prosody or naturalness. No background noise is added just to make beginner tasks harder.

## Learner experience
One activity at a time. German reading text is always available; English help is optional. Listening starts without a visible transcript; reveal is available and recorded as support. Replays are allowed. Selecting slower audio counts as support, while still requiring listening before checking. Feedback quotes a relevant paragraph or offers audio replay from the relevant segment. Later levels add sequencing, matching and an optional self-checked summary; summary prose is not automatically graded.

Completion records work done. First attempts without help are separate from assisted and repeated results. Any opening of help is remembered even after reload. Delay uses the later first-check time of the two initial activities. Learning dates and scores are client-reported practice data, not exam security. Account isolation uses the existing authenticated progress service and owner checks. Older course scores and completion are preserved.

## Validation before publication
Type checks, content/answer/evidence consistency, actual audio duration and nonempty samples, progress merge/retry/support/delay tests and representative rendered routes. Teacher and learner validation remains outstanding.
