# Zwischen Hörsaal und Arbeitswelt

A complete 200-page B1-oriented fictional reader follows Amir through finding student work, café shifts, a software student job, exams, a dissertation, graduation, rejected graduate applications and his first month of full-time work in Germany. Ten chapters contain twenty pages each. The manuscript has 33,906 German whitespace words, four paragraphs per page and 800 exact-source English paragraph translations. Every sentence remains within thirty whitespace words; each page contains 137–175 words. These editorial limits do not certify CEFR level or demonstrate learning effectiveness. Independent teacher review and learner testing remain necessary.

The B1 shelf displays one cover and title. The book opens at `/books/b1/zwischen-hoersaal-und-arbeitswelt`, with its own chapter contents and resume link. The four source paragraphs, one page-wide translation control, contextual word meanings and continuous page recording stay inside the reader. There are no comprehension questions or completion scores. B1 bookmarks use a separate volume record; existing A1/A2 bookmarks and user data remain intact.

`content/books/zwischen-hoersaal-und-arbeitswelt/manuscript.psv` is the directly authored source. `scripts/prepare-b1-book.mjs --complete` validates its structure and compiles the book, exact-source translations and evidenced character voice plans. `scripts/prepare-b1-book-glosses.mjs` applies the reviewed word overrides and checks full word coverage. The original inherited glossary contained incorrect entries; this book corrects them locally without changing other content.

Narration uses the same established synthetic male and female Qwen reference recordings as the other books and Stories. Female narration remains consistent. Explicit attributed speech follows character identity; notices and narrative prose use the narrator. Each page has one continuous Opus recording with full source-matched word starts. Validation checks source and voice-plan hashes, audio format, duration, timings and whole-recording ASR coverage of at least 90% with no more than 12% excess. `--retry-asr-without-hotwords` retries the same waveform when recognition hints interfere; it does not weaken thresholds or trim the story. Automated checks do not mean all recordings were manually listened to.

New B1 audio and timing objects are delivered separately through the Sites `BUCKET` R2 binding at `/media/books/zwischen-hoersaal-und-arbeitswelt/<asset>`. Existing recordings stay where they were. `scripts/prepare-b1-book-release.mjs --apply` requires all 200 sources, translations, meanings and validated recordings before producing an exact-hash allowlist. The HTTP route permits only listed immutable objects, supports GET/HEAD, conditional caching and single byte ranges for seeking, and rejects out-of-bounds ranges. It does not expose bucket listing, arbitrary keys or user data.

A temporary server-only upload secret authenticates PUT requests with HMAC-SHA256 signatures bound to the exact key, expiry, length and checksum. Bodies are bounded and hash checked before a conditional non-overwriting write. Upload signatures expire within fifteen minutes. The uploader receives the secret through hidden stdin, verifies all local bytes, uploads idempotently and downloads every stored object to verify its full SHA-256. Its non-secret receipt must match all 400 validated audio/timing objects before the reader can be activated. The temporary upload secret is then removed so upload requests are disabled in the published reader. The website archive contains no duplicate B1 media payload.

The fictional story is not a legal, immigration, tax or financial guide. Work permission, social insurance and employer selection are separate processes. Amir checks his own conditions with the relevant offices. The book teaches no changing numeric thresholds, promises no automatic residence extension and guarantees no employment outcome. Primary sources and the editorial brief are listed in the book's `EDITORIAL.md`.

The delivery manifest is object-manifest.json. Delivery filenames include twelve characters of the exact file SHA-256 in addition to the source-text and voice-plan hashes. The registry pins the complete checksum, length, MIME type and original local filename. Audio and timing bytes are independently addressed; regeneration cannot silently overwrite an existing cached recording. The original generation manifest remains unchanged for source and ASR checkpoint validation.
